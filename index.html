Appley Park · Ryde

Old Enough to Hear Years

High above, birds cross the open sky. For a moment I envy their freedom. Then I look again at the tree, rooted deeply, weathered beautifully, still reaching towards everything it cannot hold. And suddenly I envy that too. Perhaps that is the ache inside all of us. To have somewhere that holds us deeply, and still leave enough of ourselves reaching towards the impossible blue.
